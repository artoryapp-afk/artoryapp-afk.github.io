My Perfect Museum

Keep track of the artworks that moved you.

An artwork caught your eye at an exhibition? Take a photo, note what it made you feel, and find it again whenever you like. My Perfect Museum is your personal visit notebook: simple, and with no account to create.

Free Android app · English and French

Everything you need, nothing more

Add an artwork in seconds

Photograph the artwork or import it from your gallery. Scan the label: the app reads the text and suggests the artist and title, right on your phone. The exhibition, place, date and the people you're with carry over from one artwork to the next.

Keep what matters to you

Your impressions, the label text, your own tags such as "Favourite". Several photos per artwork, and your favourite museum suggested by default.

Find everything easily

Your collection sorted by exhibition, date, place or artist, and search across all your text: title, artist, exhibition, place, impressions, label.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored on your phone. No account, no ads. If you wish, a backup to your own Google Drive, with restore on a new phone.

At a glance

Your privacy comes first

  • No account to create: we don't know who you are.
  • Your photos and notes stay on your phone, never on our servers.
  • No ads, no data selling.
  • Label reading happens on your phone.
